## M5.1 — zero-cost: the free wins and the honesty layer

No credentials. Nothing here depends on a probe.

1. **`attachment` resolver + provenance.** `resolveCapability` accepts only
   `tools | structured | reasoning`; `attachment` is pooled by `models-dev.ts`
   and then discarded. 113 stored `attachment` values have **zero** provenance
   rows. Ollama's detail endpoint must NOT be consulted — its vocabulary cannot
   express attachment, so its silence carries no information. Closes
   go/`hy3-preview` for free (unanimous pooled `false`).
2. **Conflict visibility.** `settle()` resolves seller disagreement to unknown
   and the disagreement is then dropped: `enrich.ts` never reads
   `intrinsic.conflicts` and no conflict table exists. **102 of 116** active
   models carry at least one. Needs a table holding both sources and both
   values, an API field, and a diagnostics surface, so a `—` caused by conflict
   is distinguishable from a `—` nobody published.
3. **Provenance completion.** Split the single `cost` fact into `billingKind`,
   `effectivePrice` and `referencePrice` — three facts with three sources.
   Add the contract fields `model_facts` lacks: `source_url`,
   `resolver_version`, `probe_version`, evidence/confidence state, raw-evidence
   hash.
4. **Remove the two latent cross-provider leaks.** `resolveContext` →
   `canonical.contextLength` and `resolveMaxOutput` →
   `canonical.maxCompletionTokens` import another seller's serving limit for a
   provider-specific field. Currently inert (0 rows) and forbidden by acceptance
   criterion 6.
5. **`included` → `notApplicable`** in the VO dimension set, with the weight
   redistributed over the applicable dimensions and a methodology version bump.
6. **Identity + recorded reasons.** The two approved overlay entries with their
   re-verifiable reasons; `identity_review` rows for the two `qwen3.5-plus`
   ambiguities; and a recorded, machine-readable reason for every remaining
   unrated row so `—` is always accountable.

## M5.2 — probes (needs credentials, from environment only)

11 probes, 9 models, 3 fields — the table in the audit is the whole surface.
`MAX_PROBE_REQUESTS_PER_RUN` derived from the plan, ceiling 24. Per-model
`probe_count, probe_cost_estimate, last_probe_at, probe_version, probe_result,
probe_failure` and a probe TTL. Stop on budget exhaustion, quota error, auth
failure; respect `Retry-After`. Transient failure never becomes `false`.

The catalog UI, read API, history and diagnostics must all still run with **zero
secrets present** — probes are an optional layer, never a precondition.

## M5.3 — the LMArena bridge

A second calibratable source, fitted and gated exactly as design_arena was
(`rho ≥ 0.85`, `looRmse / baselineSd ≤ 0.55`) and validated the way it will be
used. Worth ~4 rows. Fails closed: if the fit misses its gates, no value is
published and nothing earlier in M5 is affected.
